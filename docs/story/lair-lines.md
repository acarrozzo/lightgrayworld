# Lair stir lines

**Status: drafted November 5, 2026, unreviewed.** These are what a lair says while its boss is about and has not come out: the NOT HERE state of the room card's boss box. Every turn spent there on anything but the swing (a rest, a search, a pickaxe, a potion) serves the set's next line in order, pinned in the room's own yellow line slot and written to the feed like any spoken line. Attack calls the boss out instead. A defeated boss says nothing. The data is `src/lib/game-data/lair-lines.js`; the World Tool's Lines page lists each lair's set as `stir`, spoken by the boss. Every line below is my draft in Anthony's voice, for him to rewrite; edit the data directly from here on.

## Every other lair (`any`, with the boss's name filled in)

- "Something moves in the dark. Big."
- "You hear the {name} somewhere close."
- "The {name} is here. It just isn't here yet."
- "A sound you don't like. Then nothing."
- "Whatever lives here knows you're standing in it."
- "Tracks. Fresh ones."

## The Gator (013)

- "The reeds move. The wind doesn't."
- "A long shape, just under the surface. Then just mud."
- "Bubbles. Big ones."
- "The mud is warm where something was lying."

## The Master Thief (232o)
