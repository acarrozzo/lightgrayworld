# Story

The canon of Light Gray RPG as Anthony has set it, written down so every slice and every chat reads the same page. Nothing here is derived from the code; it is what the game is about. Where a point is not settled it says so. Anthony's own note applies to all of it: none of this is totally set.

Sources: the story sessions of October 17 and 18, 2026, and Anthony's older world bible, folded in where he confirmed it. Decisions about how the story is delivered are in `DECISIONS.md`. Draft line tables live under `docs/story/`.

## Two levels of truth

Everything in the game is true at two levels, and the game only ever speaks at the first.

**In the world:** gods are real, the Angel is a real angel chained by real gods, magic comes from starmetal, Wanderers are born in Room Zero.

**Zoomed all the way out:** Light Gray is a tidally locked planet modified thousands of years ago by an ancient, hyper-advanced human civilization so that people could live on it. Their work allows artificial day and night by dimming the sun, environmental stability, and the existence of starmetal. A future humanity, capable of time travel and emotionally distant, uses Light Gray as a place to experience uncertainty and emotion by vacationing through the souls of its inhabitants. NPCs and Wanderers are both these visitors, in different categories somehow. The Angel, like everything else, is a fabricated entity put there by that civilization, most likely one of its VIPs taking a vacation as the Angel. Nobody in the world knows any of this, and the game never says it. It stays distant and is used sparingly, if at all.

## The world

**Light Gray** is the planet. **Vega** is the continent the game takes place on: a continent under the sun, a small livable area with a diverse biome, your home. Other continents exist and are reached much later in the game:

- **Fables**, a massive continent to the east. Ruled by an Emperor or Empress, known for advanced magical traditions, home of the Ultra Mage Alexis. Rejects Vega's and Domus's claims to the world.
- **Kalax Oobi**, a jungle continent to the west.
- **Domus**, the floating continent. Possibly a moon or large asteroid, home to the largest known starmetal deposit, seat of High King Bo of the Domus Kingdom, reachable only by advanced magic; ordinary people do not understand what it is.
- **Luna Gardens**, the twilight continent at the world's horizon.

There is no unified global authority. Vega and Domus both claim sovereignty over the world. Oceans separate Vega and Fables. Each continent deals mostly with its own crises.

**Star City** is Vega's capital, an isolated fortress built into the mountain on the mountain shore, the shore to the west, over the greatest starmetal mine in Vega. A massive star fragment lies under it. Long dangerous paths through forest and ridge lead to it. Camp Hero is on the Star City map. Inside the city it is illegal to speak positively of Sol.

### Gods

**Sol**, God of Light, life and clarity, worshiped by Solarians. **Ill**, Dragon God of Darkness, truth and uncertainty, worshiped by Illarians. In the world they are real. Red Town is a melting pot, open to beliefs, where people argue about faith. Star City is Illarian by law.

### Magic

It is common for people to think a chunk of a star landed long ago and that this is why there is magic. They argue about the details and some people have completely different ideas, just like real life. Nobody has the answer in the game.

**Starmetal**, also called cobalt, is the essential magical resource. It conducts magical energy. Every mage requires it. Overuse chips and destroys starmetal artifacts. It is found in Vega's mountains and in Domus's core. Very little if any is found in the Neverending Mine anymore.

**Mages** are the people who can channel starmetal-based magic, less than one percent of the population, graded Class C (minor), B (noticeable), A (elite, combat-capable) and S, the Ultra Mages: exceptionally rare, era-defining.

**Wanderers** need no starmetal. Everyone generally knows what Wanderers are. Some like them and some hate them. They get preferential treatment and for the most part they live after dying, so people envy them. A Wanderer starts out weak, as strong as a regular human, Class C, and slowly climbs: about Class B around level 30, Class A around level 100, Class S around level 150 to 200.

**Resurrection** is myth to the people of Vega; many believe mages return at sacred pillars. Wanderers mostly do come back. Vega-born mages do not.

## Room Zero

A real place. It is where Wanderers are born. They remain there until they act and press the button, which puts them into the world at the Grassy Field. Whoever made it, the first room ever made, and the pillars, is the **Maker**: nameless, never met. The signs and the narrator's voice are theirs. Nobody in the world knows the Maker or anything about the pillars, the Angel included.

## Vega's lands

- **Grassy Field.** Central, serene, where Wanderers arrive. Home to an ancient Pillar and the Destroyed Academy.
- **Red Town.** Major port town. The plain name that mostly persists. **New Babylon** is what it was called after a revival, when people fleeing a disaster of Fables' own flooded in. Red Town has its own tragedy too: the event that destroyed the Academy also killed many in Red Town. Natives and the people who came from Fables sometimes argue about whose was worse and who lost more, and about faith.
- **Forest.** Dangerous, beasts and rogues.
- **Rocky Flats and the Dwarf Village.** A mining culture beside the mountains. The Neverending Mine has almost no starmetal left.
- **Mountains.** Where the isolated Star City is.

## The guards

Each is a color and a rank.

| Color | Whose |
|---|---|
| Green | Grassy Field |
| Red | Red Town |
| Blue | Star City |
| Gray | Domus-level. Very high. |

The Blue Guard have been reduced to protecting only the far outer reaches of Star City. Dar and Radrick control the inside by their own corrupted means. The Blue Guard are torn, conflicted, and at odds with the situation. Why a Domus guard stands on a Vega road (the Savannah gate) is not known.

## The timeline

Years below are for this document only. In the game nobody gives exact years. An era is a loose term in their history for when major events changed things; no numbered eras in dialog for now.

### The poisoning, about 20 years before the game

Dar and Radrick conspire to poison the royal family and almost everyone attending a grand royal event, to claim power for themselves. Radrick becomes King as the closest remaining relative, a nephew of the former King Edward Lavelli. He shows no remorse for the death of his entire family, his parents among them. He also kills a young woman who was romantically interested in him, in an empty hallway, just because. Dar is the mastermind: he created and distributed the poison and told only a few select members of the military, who pledged allegiance to him.

Sir Frederick, the most decorated Mage Knight and an overall nice guy, survives, devastated by the loss of his daughter, Captain Eleanor Price, and of the royal family he was close to. He was a childhood friend of Edward and had secret feelings for the Queen. To protect the King's infant son, he carries the boy on the long journey to the mainland and gives him to the Academy to raise. Then he leaves for a far away land, ashamed and destroyed that he could not save everyone he loved.

The King and Queen's infant daughter is not killed. Radrick and Dar put her into the system instead. She grows up as Volphina.

The heroes of the Academy are born around this time.

### The last Sports Day, about 5 years before the game

Far from the horrid conditions of the City under Radrick, the mainland holds its annual Sports Day, where mages compete in all sorts of competition before cheering spectators. It is the last one. Radrick and Dar, aided by Volphina's teleportation magic, descend with the entire army and slaughter many mainland mages and innocents, at the Academy and in Red Town. Their intention is to kill Hopper and Alexis, the two strongest mages in the land. Dar invokes Time Stop from his artifact staff, Time Splitter; during frozen time Radrick, who is unaffected, cuts them down with his massive artifact buster sword. Hopper and Alexis are no more.

Dar and Radrick bring the darkness, and the darkness brings the scorpions. The heroes are teenagers that day. Pax is primarily responsible for saving those who survive.

Around the same time an equally horrific event happened in Fables. Fables is barely known in Vega; its effect is felt only through the people who arrived in Red Town afterward.

### The Beast Wars, the few years after

Beasts emerge across the lands the game takes place in. Pax and the other heroes fight them and set most of the current stability in the mainlands. The war is still somewhat happening: the Wanderer is fighting these beasts too.

### Now

Wanderers started appearing after Sports Day. The Guardian Angel arrived when the Wanderers did; she was not there before the Academy fell. The heroes are around twenty. Pax waits at Camp Hero for the strength of the Wanderers, to take back Star City.

Every player is a Wanderer. Any of them has the ability to become a Savior, the Eternal Mage, or possibly the Mage King themself. The ending is not decided; a fork is liked, but the content is not built.

## People

### The heroes of the Academy

Born around the poisoning, teenagers at Sports Day, about twenty now.

- **Pax.** The infant prince grown, son of the poisoned King and Queen. Ultra Mage of Light (Sol). Tall, athletic, light skin, short to medium light blond hair, calm blue eyes, white and gold. Calm, precise, naturally gifted; destined to be the next era's stabilizing figure. Waits at Camp Hero.
- **Roman (Rom).** Ultra Mage of Force. Massive, muscular, olive skin, short thick dark hair, fun and aggressive deep brown eyes, red. Brash, aggressive, heroic; unmatched physical power.
- **Lawrence (Law).** Ultra Mage of Elements. Short to medium height, slender, very dark skin, short black hair, bright optimistic blue eyes, blue. Brilliant, meta-aware, optimistic; master of arcane and elemental disciplines.
- **Eve.** Ultra Mage of Nature. Tall, strong, agile, dark olive skin, dusty brown hair, striking green eyes, green. Stealthy, sarcastic, graceful. Daughter of Alexis.
- **Judith.** Ultra Mage of Shadow. Tall, slim, ghostly, porcelain white skin, jet-black hair that turns white when enraged, icy dark eyes, black. Cunning, quiet, intimidating; deadly precision.

### The enemy

- **Radrick**, the Mage King. Nephew of Edward Lavelli, king by poison. Not an Academy mage and not a Wanderer. Class S Ultra Mage of Poison. Over seven feet tall, monstrous build, pale skin, long dark hair, bright green eyes, devilishly handsome, black light armor. Tyrant-king of Vega. The highest-level boss.
- **Dar**, War General Warlock. Mastermind of the poisoning. Master Warlock of Dark and Temporal magic, Illarian extremist, architect of the Poisoning and the Beast Wars. Old, tall, thin, warped, sickly pale, stringy black hair, gray eyes, black. Carries Time Splitter; his fight will have multiple rounds where the player is frozen. Second highest.
- **Volphina.** Teleportation magic. Elite Warlock Captain, commander of dark mage strike units, swift, ruthless, fiercely loyal to Dar. Medium height, sleek, tan skin, bright pink hair, bright violet eyes, thick pink armor. A main boss met before Radrick and Dar, third highest. She is Pax's sister, a Lavelli, and does not know it. Radrick and Dar know: they put her into the system as an infant instead of killing her, she excelled, became a general in the army as a teenager, and they brought her into their inner circle.
- **Nox and Tox**, Dar's mutated generals. **Nox**: a deranged lieutenant skilled in venom sorcery (emaciated, blotchy, patchy hair, sunken yellow eyes). **Tox**: brute force, massive, a war beast in humanoid form fueled by toxic magic (enormous, muddy green skin, bald, red feral eyes).

### The dead and the missing

- **Hopper.** Last surviving Ultra Mage of the Sixth Era, grumpy, wise, reluctant mentor to Pax. Lean, wiry, weather-tanned, scruffy white hair, tired sharp eyes. Killed at Sports Day.
- **Alexis.** Ultra Mage of Fables, mother of Eve, legendary archer and warrior, respected across continents. Tall, elegant, warm olive skin, long dark-gold hair, radiant emerald eyes. Killed at Sports Day.
- **Sir Frederick.** No one knows where he is. There are clues that he is in the Dwarf Village or the Mining Guild; at the village it is told that Frederick saved them all with his solar shield magic.
- **King Edward Lavelli**, the Queen, **Captain Eleanor Price**. Dead in the poisoning.

### Others

- **The Guardian Angel** at the Destroyed Academy. Arrived when the Wanderers did. Chained to the land by the gods, an unknown punishment. She knows who bound her and why; we never learn the details. Stoic in the face of it. Her purpose is to guide and help the Wanderer. What she needs lies deep under Star City; the Wanderer is not ready for it at first. She only half knows why she is here. She does not know the Maker or anything about the Pillar.
- **The Young Soldier.** Born on Domus. A soldier of Star City who left recently; something happened to his family and we are not sure what. He reluctantly supports the Wanderers, since they alone are required to save the city.
- **The Old Man.** His wife is dead. We never learn her name.

## Themes

Cycles of collapse and rebirth. Magic as misunderstood ancient technology. Light versus darkness as myth, not cosmic law. Youth inheriting a broken world. Competence versus corruption. A hidden advanced species observing from afar, never intruding.

## What stays a mystery

- The Maker and the pillars. Nobody in the world knows, the Angel included.
- Who bound the Angel and why.
- Why a Domus guard stands on a Vega road.
- The Despair and the Forest Princess. The Despair should be psychological as well as a place.
- The Stairway to Heaven is an unreachable room, there to add mystery on the map.

## Developer areas

Not part of the real game; they stay only while the game is not public. The Secret Arena (a stat-swap test room) and the Solar Office.

## Not now

- No Academy wings or rebuilding.
- No extra sin keys beyond Greed, Wrath and Pride.
- No exact years or numbered eras in dialog.
- The Angel's own quest, the fifth grand quest and the ending wait.

## How the story is told

Through the game's own grammar only: signs and examine actions, giver dialog that changes with the giver's own quests, rotating asides, rare placed arrival lines, item and enemy descriptions, the world feed. No cutscenes, no lore tab, no journal paragraphs. The zoomed-out truth is never spoken.

Every player-facing line matches how Anthony writes: short sentences, often fragments; second person, present tense; the instruction inside the flavor; a narrator a little bored of the player; one tic per NPC; sincerity in one sentence, never explained; dread in one line. Lines are drafted for him to rewrite before they ship.
